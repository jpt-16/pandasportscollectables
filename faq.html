Authentication & certificates

What does “hand-verified” actually mean?

An 18-point inspection carried out in Chicago on the physical piece. It covers signature ink and media analysis, substrate and surface condition, tag generation and weave structure on garments, manufacturer dating, and — for anything claimed as game-worn or game-used — photo-matching against broadcast stills. It takes nine days on average. Nothing is listed on photographs alone.

Who issues the certificate, and are they independent?

Third-party opinions are commissioned on our account rather than the consignor's, so the party paying for the opinion is not the party hoping for a particular answer. Autograph opinions go to Veritas, cards to Meridian for grading and slabbing, and the certificate itself is issued through Hallmark against a serial recorded in the Holograph ledger. We never certify a piece on our own authority alone.

What is the certificate registry?

A permanent record of every lot we have certified, held under a serial in the format PSC-0417-A9 — house prefix, lot number, inspection batch. The serial is printed on the certificate, etched on the tamper seal, and shown on the listing before you buy. The entry does not expire and is not tied to your account.

If I sell the piece on, does the certificate transfer?

Yes. Email the registrar with the serial and the new owner's details and the entry is reassigned at no charge. The authenticity guarantee below travels with it — it belongs to whoever holds the piece, not to whoever bought it from us.

What happens if a piece is later shown not to be authentic?

Full refund of the purchase price, with no time limit, to the current holder of the certificate. This applies to any lot Panda certified. We have paid it out four times in fifteen years and each case is documented in the registry against the original serial.

Do you photo-match every lot?

Only those claiming a specific game, half, or event — roughly a third of what we list. A signed piece that makes no game-worn claim gets ink and media analysis instead, because there is nothing to match it against. Where a lot is photo-matched, the listing says which footage and which frame.

Why do some lots carry a grade and others a certificate?

Trading cards are graded on a ten-point scale and sealed in a slab; the grade is the authentication. Garments, gear, balls and photographs are certified instead, because no meaningful condition scale exists for them. We list cards below 8.0 only when the card itself is scarce enough to matter.

Checking a certificate before you buy

Every listing shows its serial above the price. Quote it to the registrar at registry@pandasports.co and you'll get the inspection date, the issuing authenticator, and the current recorded holder — whether or not you bought the piece from us.

Group two

Shipping & insurance

What does shipping cost?

Free and fully insured on any lot over $500 within the United States. Below that it is a flat $18. International shipping is quoted at checkout against the crate dimensions, and the crate itself is never charged for.

How is a lot packed?

Garments ship flat in acid-free tissue inside a rigid double-wall carton. Framed pieces, bats, and anything rigid get a crate built to the piece on our own crating floor, with foam cut on the day it ships, corner blocks, and a humidity indicator card inside the glazing. Slabbed cards ship in a sealed case inside a void-filled carton.

Do you ship worldwide?

Yes, to any country our carriers serve. Crating is included at no cost. Import duties and taxes are the buyer's responsibility and are not collected by us — we declare the full sale value, because under-declaring voids the transit insurance.

How long before it ships?

Two business days for anything already boxed. Five to seven for framed or oversize lots, because the crate is built after you buy rather than kept in stock. You get the tracking number and a photograph of the packed crate before it leaves.

Is it insured in transit?

For the full sale value, under the carrier's cover and our own policy behind it. Signature on delivery is required above $1,000 and cannot be waived. If a carrier leaves a lot unattended against those instructions, the claim is ours to make, not yours.

Can I collect in person?

Yes, at 1420 W. Fulton Market, Chicago, Tuesday to Friday by appointment. Bring photo identification matching the order. Collection is also the only way to inspect a lot under the bench lamp before you commit — we encourage it.

Do you frame pieces to order?

We do, using UV-filtering glazing and archival mounts, with the certificate mounted in a sealed pocket on the reverse. It adds three to four weeks and is quoted per piece. Nothing is glued, taped, or pinned through fabric.

Group three

Returns & damage claims

What is the returns window?

Fourteen days from delivery, on every lot. There is no reduced window for higher-value pieces and no category excluded from it. We do not run sales, so there are no final-sale items to carve out.

What condition does it need to come back in?

As it arrived, with the tamper seal unbroken and the certificate included. A slabbed card must still be sealed. If the seal is broken the piece has to be re-inspected before we can resell it, and that cost comes out of the refund — so open the crate, not the seal.

Who pays return shipping?

We do, if the lot is not as described — including any discrepancy between the listing and the inspection notes. For a change of mind it is $18 domestic on a label we issue, or the actual crating cost on an oversize piece, deducted from the refund.

It arrived damaged. What now?

Photograph the crate before unpacking any further, then email claims@pandasports.co within 48 hours of delivery. Keep all packing material — the carrier will ask for it. We file the claim ourselves and settle with you directly rather than making you wait on the carrier: replacement where an equivalent lot exists, full refund where it does not, within ten business days of your email.

When does the refund land?

Three business days after the piece is back on the bench and checked against its inspection notes. It returns to the original payment method. Wire payments are refunded by wire, and we cover the outbound fee.

Group four

Payment & financing

How can I pay?

Major cards and ACH for anything up to $25,000. Above that we require a bank wire, which is a fraud-control measure rather than a preference. We do not accept cryptocurrency or cash on collection.

Do you offer instalments?

A four-month plan at zero interest on lots between $1,000 and $25,000, taken in equal monthly payments. The piece stays in the vault, insured, until the final payment clears, and the certificate is issued in your name from the first. Missing a payment releases the lot back to the floor and refunds what you have paid, less a $50 handling charge.

Will you hold a lot for me?

For 48 hours, once per lot, by email to the floor. We do not extend holds and we do not run a waitlist behind them — if the hold lapses the lot goes straight back up.

Can I make an offer?

On lots that have been listed thirty days or more, yes. Not on new arrivals, and not on anything from the current Thursday drop. Where a consignor has set a reserve we will tell you that an offer cannot be accepted rather than let you keep bidding against a floor you cannot see.

What about sales tax?

Illinois sales tax applies to lots delivered in state. Elsewhere it is collected where we have economic nexus, calculated at checkout before you confirm. Export sales are zero-rated; import charges at the destination are separate and yours.
