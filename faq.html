Group one

Authenticity & certificates

What does “authenticated” mean here?

That an independent authenticator has examined the piece and issued a certificate for it — not us. We buy stock that is already authenticated, then check each item against its certificate when it arrives: that the serial matches, that the item is what the paperwork describes, and that its condition is what we're about to claim. Nothing is listed from photographs alone.

Who does the authentication?

Never us — that's the point. Authentication comes from the established third-party authenticators and graders the trade uses, and each listing names the one behind that piece. A certificate written by the person selling you the item is worth very little, so we don't write any.

Does my item come with a certificate?

Yes — the original third-party certificate ships with the item. Keep the two together: separated from its certificate, a signed piece is worth a fraction of what it was, and no replacement we could write would fix that.

What if an item turns out not to be authentic?

You get your money back in full. If you have reason to think a piece isn't what we sold it as, email us with what you've found and we'll deal with it rather than argue about it. set any time limit on this

How do graded cards work?

They arrive in their sealed slab, exactly as graded. The grade is on the listing and on the slab itself. Don't break the seal — an opened slab loses the grade, and with it most of the reason the card is worth what it is.

Can I ask questions about a piece before I buy?

Please do. Email us and we'll send extra photographs, close-ups of the signature or tags, and whatever we know about where the piece came from. There's no pressure attached to asking.

If it isn't authentic, you get your money back

That covers every item we sell as authentic, and it isn't a promotion or a limited offer — it's the deal. If you have reason to think a piece isn't what we said it was, email us with what you've found and we'll sort it out rather than argue about it.

Group two

Shipping

What does shipping cost?

It's calculated at checkout from the destination and the size of the item, and shown in full before you confirm. add your rates and any free-shipping threshold

How is my order packed?

Garments ship flat in tissue inside a rigid box. Cards ship sleeved and immobilised in a void-filled carton. Framed and rigid items are packed to suit the piece rather than to suit a standard box. Nothing is folded that shouldn't be folded.

Do you ship internationally?

confirm which countries you ship to Where we do ship internationally, import duties and taxes are the buyer's responsibility and are charged by your country, not by us.

How soon does it ship, and can I track it?

You get a tracking number by email as soon as it's on its way. state your dispatch time

Is my order insured in transit?

confirm your insurance and signature-on-delivery terms If a carrier loses or damages something, sorting it out is our job, not yours — see the damage question below.

Group three

Returns & damage

Can I return something?

Yes. set your returns window The same terms apply to every item — we don't run sales, so there's nothing marked final-sale to catch you out.

What condition does it need to come back in?

As it arrived, with the certificate included and any grading slab still sealed. Unpack the box, not the piece — once a slab is opened or a certificate is separated from its item, neither of us can put it back the way it was.

Who pays return shipping?

We do, if the item isn't what the listing said it was. For a change of mind, state who pays

It arrived damaged. What now?

Photograph the box before you unpack any further, then email us with the pictures. Keep all the packing material — the carrier will want to see it. We handle the carrier claim ourselves and settle with you directly, rather than making you wait on them: a replacement where an equivalent piece exists, a full refund where it doesn't.

When does the refund come through?

Once the item is back with us and checked, to the payment method you used. state your processing time

Are there fees on top of the listed price?
