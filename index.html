How authentication works

Three gates, in order

A lot that fails any one of them never reaches the floor. Roughly one in six pieces we're offered is returned to the consignor.

  1. I

    Sourced

    Pieces come from the athlete, their estate, or consignors we've worked with for years. Provenance is written down — who held it, when, and how it left the field — before we agree to anything.

    Chain of custody on file

  2. II

    Verified

    An 18-point inspection: photo-matching against broadcast footage, ink and media analysis on every signature, plus tag, weave and manufacturer dating on garments and gear.

    18-point inspection · 9 days average

  3. III

    Certified

    The lot is serialized, tamper-sealed, and entered in the registry with its inspection photographs. Your certificate stays searchable for life — and transfers when you sell it on.

    Lifetime registry entry
