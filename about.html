About us

Collecting shouldn't be out of reach

Panda Sports Collectibles sells authentic sports memorabilia at prices ordinary fans can actually pay. Four brothers, one shop, and a deliberately short list of promises. We're new — we'd rather say so than imply a history we don't have — so this page is about how we work, which is the part you can judge us on from day one.

Why we started

Two problems, both fixable

Sports memorabilia has two problems that keep ordinary fans out of it. The first is that you often can't tell what you're buying — a certificate is only as good as whoever issued it, and plenty are issued by the person doing the selling. The second is price: between auction premiums, buyer's fees and estimates set high on purpose, a piece can cost half again what it should before anyone has shipped anything.

Neither is hard to fix. You authenticate what you sell, properly, and you say who did it. You price the piece once and you don't add fees on top. Neither of those requires being a large company — it mostly requires deciding not to do it the other way.

So that's what Panda is. A shop for people who want a signed jersey on the wall or a graded rookie card in a case, who want to know it's real, and who don't want to remortgage anything to get one.

We're at the start of this. There's no decade of trading to point at, no wall of testimonials, and we're not going to manufacture either. What we can show you is what each piece is, what documentation comes with it, and what it costs with nothing added at checkout — and that's true on the first order as much as the thousandth.

On where the stock comes from: we buy through established auction houses and dealers in the memorabilia trade. What we won't do is write our own certificates — paperwork from the person selling you the item is worth very little, which is half of what's wrong with this market in the first place. Whatever documentation a piece carries, that's what the listing will tell you it carries.

What we will and won't list

  • Items we have in hand before they go up
  • Pieces whose documentation we can describe honestly
  • A clear price, with no fees added at checkout
  • Anything we'd have to overstate to sell
  • Certificates issued by whoever is selling the piece
  • Replicas or reprints described as anything else

What we promise

Four things, in plain terms

Short list, deliberately. We'd rather commit to a few things and keep them.

  • We've held everything we sell

    Nothing is drop-shipped and nothing is listed from a photograph someone else took. Stock arrives here, gets checked against its paperwork, and ships from here.

  • If it isn't authentic, you get your money back

    Any item we sell as authentic that turns out not to be is refunded in full. That's not a promotion, it's the basic deal.

  • The price is the price

    No buyer's premium, no auction fees, no estimate inflated so a discount can be applied later. What's on the listing is what you pay, plus shipping.

  • We tell you what we actually know

    Listings say what documentation a piece carries and where it came from. Where there's a gap in what we know, the listing says so instead of papering over it.

Have a look around

Have a look at what's in stock. If you have a question about a piece before you buy, ask — you'll get a real person, usually the same day.
